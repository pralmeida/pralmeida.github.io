Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

mcp-home is a personal application used only by its author, to access the author's own Google accounts. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the author's explicit consent, the app uses the Gmail API scopes gmail.readonly (read email) and gmail.compose (create drafts) on the author's own accounts.

How data is used and stored

Email content is read on demand and returned only to the author's own AI assistant session. The app does not store email content. OAuth refresh tokens are stored on the author's own server and are not shared.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train models by this app. Use of Google user data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
